5. Contacting us

The enquiry form does not send any data itself. It opens your email program with a prepared message to us. If you write to us, we process the information you provide (such as name, company, email address, telephone number and your message) in order to answer your enquiry and, where applicable, to prepare a quotation.

The legal basis is the performance of pre-contractual measures (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) or our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We store the data for as long as is necessary to process your enquiry or for as long as statutory retention obligations apply.
