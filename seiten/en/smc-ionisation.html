The problem

Static electricity costs quality.

Electrostatic charge can affect production processes, cause material to stick, increase rejects or impair sensitive components.

With the right ionisation systems, static electricity can be neutralised in a targeted way.

Material sticking

Films and plastic parts stick to each other or to tools.

More rejects

Dust is attracted, parts are fed incorrectly, processes become unstable.

Sensitive components

Electronic components can be impaired by discharge.
