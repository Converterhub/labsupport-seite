On-site service

Maintenance, repair and calibration

In the event of a fault, fast and directly on site at your premises – also for systems from various manufacturers.

An analytical system only delivers reliable results if it is in good technical order. We take care of the maintenance, repair and calibration of your analytical systems and also maintain gas generators and dryers. Because service and qualification are handled by a single provider, there is no need to coordinate between different service providers – for example for a requalification after an intervention.

We support chromatography and elemental analysis systems in pharmaceutical and GMP laboratories, in the chemical industry and in quality control, research and development. In addition, we have many years of experience with gas analysers for process and exhaust gas analysis, particularly in engine and fuel cell development.

Analytical instruments Laboratory

  • Chromatography – high-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC), gas chromatography (GC), GC-mass spectrometry (GC-MS)
  • Elemental analysis – ICP mass spectrometry (ICP-MS), ICP optical emission spectroscopy (ICP-OES), atomic absorption spectroscopy (AAS)
  • Spectroscopy and gas analysis – photometers, infrared spectrometry, flame ionisation (FID), magnetopneumatics, quantum cascade lasers (QCL)
  • Pharmaceutical testing – dissolution, disintegration and particle testers

Generators & dryers Systems

  • Gas generation – N₂ and H₂ generators, synthetic air systems
  • Compressed air drying – refrigerated and adsorption dryers

Our approach Process

  1. Contact – you describe the instrument, the fault symptoms or the maintenance requirement. Often the first conversation is enough to narrow down what needs to be done.
  2. Assessment and appointment – we estimate the effort and spare parts required and agree on an appointment, as quickly as possible in the event of a fault.
  3. On-site visit – maintenance, repair or calibration directly in your laboratory, coordinated with your laboratory operations.
  4. Report and completion – you receive a traceable service report. For GMP systems, we can also carry out the subsequent requalification on request.
